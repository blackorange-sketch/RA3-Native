### Fixes

- **Large mods no longer run out of memory:** big mods such as *Generals Evolution* stopped while loading a map with `guest heap exhausted` (about 1.5 GB). On PC players of such mods use the "4 GB patch"; the port now does the same, and the game's memory can grow past that limit. Tested with *Generals Evolution 0.3.3*: 28-30 fps in battle at 1080p, Ultra (Snapdragon 8 Gen 2).
