### What's new

- **Drag the map** (launcher option): the spot you touch stays under your finger, like in a map app or a mobile strategy game, and a quick swipe is finished after you let go. Off: the camera moves the way you swipe, as before.
- **Button size and opacity** (launcher options): on-screen buttons from 80 to 200% in size and from 30 to 100% opacity.
- **Move the buttons anywhere:** in the button editor (`EDIT`) hold any button — also ⌨ and `EDIT` itself — and drag it to a new place; `RESET` puts them all back. Remove a button with its red corner.
- **Mouse buttons** in the editor (`KEYS 2`): with `RMB` lit a tap is a right click; with `LMB` lit a drag draws a selection box at once.
- **Game on a memory card or USB drive:** the launcher also looks for the game on memory cards and USB (OTG) drives, and **Game folder** lists the other storages at the top.
