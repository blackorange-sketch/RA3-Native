### What's new

- **Wide screen resolutions in the game's options.** With *Wide screen* on, the resolution list in the game's graphics options now offers sizes in your screen's own shape (for example 1536×720, 2306×1080 and the full screen size on a 21:9 phone), and the current one is shown correctly. A resolution picked there is kept after a restart. Before, the list only had 16:9 sizes, showed a wrong value and could switch the game back to 16:9.
- With *Wide screen* off, the list shows 16:9 sizes only.
- **Pause menu in wide screen:** during a battle the sides of the screen keep showing the battle (no bars), also when the pause menu is open.
