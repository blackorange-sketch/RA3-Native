### What's new

- **Wide screen.** A new launcher option, *Wide screen*, lets battles fill the whole screen of modern phones instead of a 16:9 picture, so you see more of the map. The game's resolution is set to your screen's shape automatically on every start.
  - The on-screen buttons (keyboard, `EDIT` and your own hotkeys) move to one row at the top centre, between the top-left panel and the minimap.
  - Menus, loading screens and cutscenes stay 16:9 as the game designed them. In menus and on loading screens the sides show a blurred copy of the picture in the game's colours; cutscenes keep black sides.
  - Off by default. If you change the resolution in the game's options, restart the game to get the wide picture back.
- **Smaller, see-through buttons.** The on-screen buttons are a bit smaller, with thinner frames and letters, and their dark fill lets the map show through. The button editor stays opaque.
