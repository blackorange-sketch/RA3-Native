### What's new

- **Draw merging:** identical buildings, vehicles and trees that stand in different places are now drawn together in one GPU call (instancing). Roughly 300–400 fewer driver calls per frame and about 20% less render thread time than 0.2.4 — less heat and battery drain, more headroom on weaker phones. `/sdcard/ra3_nobatch` turns it off.
- The render thread is woken less often, which takes a little more work off the game thread.

### Fixes

- With an enlarged HUD, messages and tooltip texts are no longer drawn twice with an offset: texts now move with the panel under them.
