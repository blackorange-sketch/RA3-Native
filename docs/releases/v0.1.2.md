### What's new

- **HUD size** — new launcher option (100–200%, 110% by default) that enlarges the minimap, build menu, portrait and other corner panels together with their texts, tooltips and the commander video. Taps land on the enlarged buttons. Menus and windows in the middle of the screen stay as they are.
- **New loading screen** with the launcher's look and a progress bar.
- **Changing the resolution in the game's options** no longer leaves the picture in one corner.
- **Mali / PowerVR (experimental):** DXT textures are decoded on the CPU, large ones at half resolution to save memory. Reports from Mali devices are welcome.
- The project is now licensed under **GPL-3.0**.
